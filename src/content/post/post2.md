We are pleased to announce that the second session of the Early Chinese Legal Documents Workshop will focus on “The Case of Ye Abducting Ni” 夜略尼案 from the Eastern Han Dynasty Bamboo Slips from Wuyi Square, Changsha. All interested participants are warmly invited.

**Date & Time**  
- September 26, 2025, 9:00–11:00 AM (U.S. Eastern Time)  
- Online via Zoom: [Join here](https://simmons.zoom.us/j/99524091104)  

**Discussion Leader**  
- Yunxin Li (Simmons University)
