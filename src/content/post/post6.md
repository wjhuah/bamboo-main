We are pleased to announce that the sixth session of the **Early Chinese Legal Documents Workshop** will discuss three strips associated with a single arson case titled *“Fei Zonghuo Shi Shan Zonghuo”* 非縱火時擅縱火 from the Zoumalou Western Han bamboo slips. All interested participants are welcome to attend.

**Date & Time**
- March 6, 2026, 8:30 AM (U.S. Eastern Time)
- Online via Zoom: [Join here](https://harvard.zoom.us/j/8617565346)

**Discussion Leader**
- Hwei Ru Ong (Harvard University)
