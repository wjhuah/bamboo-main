We are pleased to announce that the fourth session of the **Early Chinese Legal Documents Workshop** will focus on the translation of Eastern Han manuscripts from May First Square (*Wuyi guangchang*), with particular attention to entries related to fire incidents. All interested participants are warmly invited to join.

**Date & Time**  
- January 16, 2026, 8:30–10:30 AM (U.S. Eastern Standard Time, EST)
- Online via Zoom: [Join here](https://notredame.zoom.us/j/98079885982)
