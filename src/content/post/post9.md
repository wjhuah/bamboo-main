We are pleased to announce that the ninth session of the **Early Chinese Legal Documents Workshop** will focus on the case titled *“Wuyang Xiang Sefu Xiangren Lian Cong An”* 無陽鄉嗇夫襄人斂賨案, preserved in the Western Han bamboo slips from Zoumalou, Changsha. All interested participants are invited to join.

**Date & Time**  
- June 5, 2026, 9:30 AM (U.S. Eastern Time)  
- Online via Zoom: [Join here](https://harvard.zoom.us/j/8617565346)

**Discussion Leader**  
- Yifan Zheng (University of Washington)
- Hwei Ru Ong (Harvard University)
