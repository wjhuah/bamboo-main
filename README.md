## 📌 Overview & Leadership

The **Early Chinese Legal Documents Workshop** (早期中國法律文獻研讀坊) is an academic initiative and reading group series for scholars and PhD students worldwide working with primary sources of early Chinese legal history, with a special focus on legal cases and manuscripts unearthed from Qin and Han tombs.

* **Organizers:** Liang Cai (University of Notre Dame) & Yunxin Li (Simmons University)
* **Website Maintainer:** Wenjie Hua (Georgetown University)
* **Schedule:** Every two weeks online on Friday, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM (U.S. Eastern Time)
* **Language:** Bilingual (English and Mandarin / 中英雙語)

---

## 📑 Workshop Format

1. **Pre-circulated Materials:** One week prior to each session, the speaker distributes reading materials (20–50 pages), including:
   * High-resolution images or page references of original bamboo/wooden slips (簡牘圖版)
   * Chinese transcriptions (釋文)
   * English translation and detailed scholarly notes (英文翻譯與考釋)
   * Relevant bibliography and literature review
2. **Active Discussion:** Participants read in advance and actively engage in comments and discussions during the session.
3. **Publication:** Notes are revised after group discussions and prepared for future publication.
